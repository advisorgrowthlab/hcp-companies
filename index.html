The systems that run it every month

Compliance packaging that fits how your team reviews

Built from your story, so it sounds like you.

Your compliance team reviews and approves. We do the rest.

That means your compliance team, broker-dealer or insurance carrier.

  1. We pre-check every page, post, ad and script before it's submitted, against the rules that apply to you (FINRA's communications rules, the SEC Marketing Rule or your carrier's advertising standards) and your firm's own policies.
  2. Your team gets a ready-to-submit package: the final copy, screenshots or the video, the disclosures and notes on any claims.
  3. We submit one small piece at a time, because short submissions tend to get through faster.
  4. If anything comes back, we make the changes and repackage it.

Nothing goes live until your firm signs off. Once it's approved, we post through the tools your firm approves, so it's archived the way your firm requires.

How it works.

12-month plans. The podcast and The Lab run on a 12-month commitment, because a show takes time to find its audience and a plan needs a full year to prove itself.

Bundles cost less. Combining pieces, such as the podcast with The Lab, costs less than buying each one alone.

Referrals earn you free services. When an advisor you refer signs on with us, you earn free services, as long as your firm's policies allow you to accept them.

Your agency can sponsor. Your agency pays, you pay, or you split it, and sponsoring several advisors brings the cost per advisor down.
